Yet again, I would like to thank you all for coming to this year's Picocon.

I would like to thank everyone who submitted an entry for the Wyrmtongue anthology. For those who are new, this a collection of short stories and artwork, usually related to the year's theme, compiled for you all to read. I hope you enjoy!
