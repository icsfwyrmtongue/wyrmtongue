Hello all! Welcome to Picocon 43. This is your Sofa, Rajveer, a second year physicist. This year's theme is Wyrmhole, sounds mysterious doesn’t it? Any guesses as to its origin? 

It’s just a pun on wormholes…

The idea was to have a theme covering sci-fi and fantasy with significant elements involving Space. But condensing that into a singular word was difficult. Thankfully, this year’s Chair of Vice, Michael, came up with a cool name.

After all, Wyrmhole brings together two ideas that show up constantly in science fiction and fantasy: wyrms, an old name for dragons, and holes of all kinds, from black holes, wormholes, and portals to the interdimensional rift.

In the morning, we will begin with a talk from Drew Wagar is RSM 228, where he will talk about “Building Worlds:”

“I’ll be taking the audience behind the scenes of the Shadeward Saga (with a few side quests into Elite) to show how a single idea developed into a living, internally consistent universe. Blending hard science with mythic storytelling, I’ll explore how world-building begins with asking the right questions — and how imagination, discipline and curiosity can grow into entire civilisations.”

After this, Paul McAuley will give a talk about portals in sci-fi and how he explored these and other concepts in the Jackaroo series.

Next, we will break for lunch at about 12:00. We will separate to get food on campus or from the various restaurants in South Kensington. Feel free to ask the committee for any recommendations.

Around 12:30, we will begin the Baguette Bash (on Queen’s Lawn hopefully). Get ready to fight!
At 13:00, we will run our signature event, Destruction of Dodgy Merchandise (DoDM) in CAGB 103, where we will bid to save or smash dodgy merch frozen in liquid nitrogen. This event is now taking place this year, even after some earlier signs that it might not be possible.


We will then return to RSM 228 for a Q\&A panel with our Guests of Honor at 14:00. After this, we will end the day with some trivia, the signature “Turkey Talks” –  where you can talk about just whatever you want but remember we can control how long you can speak for via donations – and other smaller events.

Don’t forget about the scavenger hunt either, which will be running in parallel throughout the day. Femtocon will also be ongoing, with games borrowed from boardgame society, so look out for that.

I would like to thank you all to coming to Picocon and hope you all have a great time.
