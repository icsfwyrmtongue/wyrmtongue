Before writing full-time, Paul McAuley earned a PhD in Botany and worked as a researcher in multiple top British universities. 

Over a thirty-year career, he has written multiple award-winning books that showcase his incredible range.

His work includes: \textit{Fairyland}, winner of both the Arthur C. Clarke and John W. Campbell Awards, a which exploring biohacking and genetic engineering; \textit{Cowboy Angels}, his ambitious take on alternate history and time-travel; and most recently, \text{Loss Protocol}, in which a ranger becomes entangled with a cult that believes the world can be healed through the power of collective dreams.


% Paul McAuley, biologist and writer of hard science fiction
