Author of multiple best-selling science fiction and fantasy novels, Drew Wagar has also written novels set in the video-game universes of Elite Dangerous and Lords of Midnight. 

Drew also has a Youtube channel under his own name, where he showcases his many interests, with streams and lore videos on Elite Dangerous standing out. 

His original work includes the Shadeward and Hegira sagas, each taking place over a galactic scope, backed by a real love for the sci-fi concepts within.
