Wyrmholes are many things. A synthesis of wyrms and wormholes. An incredible pun. Dragons tearing open gashes in the space-time fabric of the universe is an inherently awesome (in the biblical sense) image, and a good synthesis between the scifi and fantasy elements of the club. Having been banished to Milton Keynes (a place as monotone as a singularity), I have pondered much on the essence of what a wyrmhole exactly is… and have come to the revelation that it is, in a word, “cool”. Although it has been harder than expected to help from my distant abode, I hope you all enjoy this years Picocon!
